Jade File Encryption

Lock and unlock your files with your Jade. Nothing leaves this page.

Experimental. This tool has not been audited. Do not rely on it as the only copy of anything important, and keep an unencrypted backup until you have tested restoring your wallet and decrypting a file yourself.

How this stays safe
  • The key that unlocks your files lives only in Jade, derived from your wallet. Restore the same wallet on any Jade and your files open again.
  • Unlocking Jade with your PIN is the approval. While Jade is unlocked and plugged in, decryption does not ask for a further tap on the device, so disconnect when you are done.
  • The page makes no network requests except relaying Jade's PIN handshake to Blockstream's pinserver while unlocking. Save this page and open it from disk to use it offline.
  • Files are standard OpenPGP, the same format GnuPG produces. If this page ever goes away, GnuPG with jade-agent can open them (see the README).
  • The key comes from your wallet seed and its BIP39 passphrase, if you use one. A wallet restored without the passphrase gets a different key and cannot open your files.
  • Works in Chrome, Edge and Brave on desktop (Web Serial). Safari and Firefox do not expose USB serial to web pages.
